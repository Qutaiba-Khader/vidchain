# Privacy

VidChain collects nothing.

- No analytics, no crash reporting to any server, no usage tracking, no account system, no developer cloud sync.
- The app talks only to the sites whose links you open or share to it, and to GitHub for the yt-dlp engine update and the in-app browser ad-block filter list
  (and, if enabled, the isolated nightly yt-dlp fallback). Nothing about you is sent anywhere else.
- Logins you make inside the in-app browser stay on your phone.

Issues and questions: https://github.com/Qutaiba-Khader/vidchain/issues
